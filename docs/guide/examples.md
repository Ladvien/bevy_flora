# The examples

Every example in the crate, and the first-person sandbox that puts the gameplay APIs together.

## Everything at once: the playground

`examples/playground.rs` is a small first-person game built on the crate, to read as an example of wiring its gameplay APIs together: walk among an apple tree, an oak and a ring of mixed trees, strike a trunk with an axe (`FloraIndex::cast_ray` finds it, `TreeNotch` deepens, the fourth blow fells it with `FellTree`), buck the fallen tree (`BuckTree`), shake a tree (`TreeShakes`) and gather the apples that fall (`FallenFruit`), pick apples off it (`TreeFruit::nearest`, `pick`), light a fire (`FloraFire`, counting `TreeBurnt`), call the rain (`FloraWetness`), move the year on (`FloraSeason::of_year`), and save and load all of it (`FellTree::lying`, `TreeFruit::with_picked`, `Burnt`, `FloraFire::restore`). Every action reads a `Controls` resource, so `--demo` plays a script through the same systems and records it.

![First person among apple trees: walking up to an apple tree, shaking it so apples fall into the grass, picking apples off it, turning to an oak, striking it four times so it falls away, stepping back in the rain as it is bucked into logs beside its stump; a counter of apples and logs at the top](../media/playground.gif)

*`cargo run --release --example playground -- --demo`; without `--demo` it is yours to play: `W` `A` `S` `D` walk, right mouse look, left click axe, `F` shake, `E` pick, `B` buck, `G` fire, `R` rain, `Y` a month on, `F5` save, `F9` load.*

## Examples

| example | what it shows |
|---|---|
| `showcase` | An interactive growing tree. Right-drag / wheel orbits, `[` `]` cycle presets, `N` reseeds, `Space` toggles growth, `R` restarts, `M` jumps to mature, `G` rebuilds, `U` switches between growing in the vertex shader (`GpuGrowth`) and rebuilding on the CPU, `C` draws the gameplay shape, `Y` runs the year (`FloraSeason::of_year`, 40 s a year), `H` hits the tree (`TreeShakes`; leaves fall), `X` strikes it with an axe (`TreeNotch`, deeper with each blow; the fourth fells it), `F` fells it (`FellTree`) and then `B` bucks it into logs (`BuckTree`); any other key brings a new one. |
| `gallery` | Renders 526 scenarios (every preset at several ages and seeds, forced splits, smoothing, pipe radii (mature and young) and a wobbly twisted trunk on every preset, every leaf style × placement × orientation, every crown shape, every preset painterly and toon, parameter extremes, pendant strands on an oak (leaning, plumb, over a raised floor, young), five deciduous presets and the pine turning, turned, half bare, bare and under snow, the cherry's spring in blossom, and sick and dying trees of eleven presets, young and in autumn) offscreen to four-view PNGs plus a `stats.tsv` with mesh sanity flags. This is the visual QA harness. `--filter <substr>` narrows it; `--gpu` grows every tree in the vertex shader. |
| `compare_gallery` | Compares two renders directory against directory (CPU `gallery` against `gallery --gpu`, or two `capture_growth` runs): mean difference and share of close pixels per PNG, non-zero exit past the limits. |
| `inspect` | Photographs one tree under controlled conditions, to see how it reads: a camera that backs off through every level of detail and into the impostor (`--distance a,b --frames n`), optionally zooming so the tree keeps its size on screen and a level switch shows as a jump (`--constant-size`), any bearing, height and field of view, one level forced at every distance (`--level`), a `ProceduralTree` instead of a pooled tree (`--mode tree`, which `--lod` or `--level` give a `TreeLod`), the empty scene (`--empty`) to measure the tree's pixels against, with or without MSAA, in any look (`--style natural|painterly|toon`, which `forest` and `capture_growth` take too) and season (`--season <autumn>,<leaf fall>[,<snow>[,<blossom>]]`, which `forest` takes too), sick or dying (`--condition sick|dying`: a `TreeCondition`; with `--decline <seconds>[,<after>[,<every>]]` the condition is set once the tree is ready and drawn as a `ConditionGlide`, photographed the same way as the next options), notched (`--notch <bearing>,<depth>[,<height>]`: an axe's `TreeNotch`), felled (`--fell <bearing>`: the photographs wait until it lies still; with `--lying` it is laid down at once, as from a save) and bucked (`--buck <m>`: into logs `m` metres long), on a slope (`--slope <deg>`, the ground falling toward +x: the tree at its footing, or with `--on-centre` where a plain placement would put it), dropping leaves (`--drop <count>[,<after>[,<every>]]`: photographed `after` seconds after they let go, then every `every` seconds), shedding them as an autumn passes (`--autumn <seconds>[,<after>[,<every>]]`: the leaf fall rises to 1 over `seconds`, photographed the same way), burning (`--burn <after>[,<every>]`: a `FloraFire` lit at the tree's foot, photographed the same way), or with a body standing in it or walking through it (`--push <x>,<z>[,<radius>[,<vx>,<vz>]]`: a red `FoliagePusher`, photographed every quarter of a second while it walks). The clock is fixed, so runs repeat. |
| `capture_growth` | Records the clips above as numbered PNG frames: one tree growing (`--preset <name>`, default maple), with `--grove` a grove of mixed species with a gust front crossing it, with `--seasons` the same grove fully grown through a year, with `--shake` the grove knocked by `TreeShakes`, with `--fell` an oak notched by an axe and felled among a few trees, with `--fire` the grove burning at dusk, with `--push` a body walking through a patch of shrubs, with `--chop` a woodcutter felling a row of trees one after another, each found by `FloraIndex`, or with `--harvest` an apple tree shaken and another picked into a basket (`--gpu` grows the trees in the vertex shader). The clock steps a fixed 1/24 s per frame, so the wind sways smoothly and two runs write the same frames; the `ffmpeg` commands that make the GIFs are in the example's docs. |
| `forest` | The forest-scale benchmark: up to 100 000 trees under a shadow-casting sun, on a grid or, with `--scatter oak:3,birch:1,...`, where `Scatter` puts them (`--grove <m>`, `--clearing <m>`, `--stand <x,z,radius>`: the last species there alone; `--undergrowth shrub:2.5` adds a patchy layer of shrubs 2.5 m apart and clear of the trunks), on flat ground or on rolling hills (`--terrain <relief>`: every tree at its footing on a `FloraGround`), drawn from a `TreePool` per species (`--mode pool`, the default; `--background-pools` builds the pools with `BackgroundPools`), as a `ProceduralTree` each (`--mode naive`), or as `GpuGrowth` trees, fully grown or growing for the whole run (`--mode gpu`, `gpu-live`; `--mode cpu-live` is the CPU-rebuilt equivalent), built in the frame or in the background (`--background`), in full or at levels of detail (`--tree-lod`: `TreeLod`), timed per frame or saved as a screenshot; the loading report gives the longest frame while the forest came in. `--offscreen` times the real frame cost without a window and the result line gives the mean, p95, median, minimum and CPU time per frame; `--no-impostors`, `--lod` and `--shadows` change the setup, `--impostor-elevation <deg>` and `--impostor-shadows` the pools' impostors, and `--shadow-radius <m>` lets only the trees that near the camera cast (`NotShadowCaster` on the rest); `--ortho <view height>` makes the camera orthographic; `--sun <x,y,z>`, `--wind <strength>`, `--wind-dir <x,z>`, `--gust <speed>` and `--wind-animate` (the strength changes every frame) move the light and the weather, `--shake-animate` knocks the trees every few frames (every shake slot busy), `--leaf-fall-animate <s>` runs an autumn every `s` seconds (leaves always falling), `--season <autumn>,<leaf fall>[,<snow>[,<blossom>]]` sets the season, `--fire <x>,<z>[,<radius>]` lights a `FloraFire` over the forest there (`--fire armed`: one that burns nothing), `--push <x>,<z>` stands a `FoliagePusher` there; `--see-through <x,y,z>[,<radius>]` stands a player in the forest for the camera to see through to (`SeeThroughTrees`) and `--prepass <none|depth|all>` gives the camera prepasses; `--sick <share>` and `--dying <share>` give that share of the trees (picked by a hash of their number) a `TreeCondition`; `--deterministic` fixes the clock at 1/60 s, so two builds' `--screenshot`s compare byte for byte. |
| `tree_file` | (`--features ron`) A tree from `examples/assets/trees/maple.tree.ron` (`--tree <path>` for another file); with `file_watcher` too it rebuilds when the file is saved, and a load error shows on screen while the last good tree stays. `--export <preset>` prints a preset as a tree file, `--screenshot <path>` saves the tree and exits. |
| `playground` | A first-person sandbox where the gameplay APIs meet: chop, buck, shake, pick, burn, soak and save the trees (see "Everything at once: the playground"); `--demo` plays and records a scripted eight seconds. |
| `isometric` | An orthographic isometric camera over a pooled forest: walk with `W` `A` `S` `D`, zoom with the mouse wheel and watch the trees hand over to impostors by the zoom, `T` turns the see-through cylinder off and on (see "Isometric and top-down games"); `--demo` plays and records a scripted eight seconds. |

cargo run --release --example showcase
cargo run --release --example gallery -- --out target/gallery/latest --filter mature_
